The Story of Bart
This is Bart's story told in BobCat's own words as they appeared in posts at KittyWhiskas.  Certain portions have been edited to reflect information that is no longer pertinent and to allow for space constraints.  BobCat's words will appear in brown print, with my own comments appearing in black italicized print.
From: bobcat_samkim
Date: Sun Dec 17, 2000 7:11 pm
Subject: Christmas spirit came today

To all:

Today I was the luckiest person around.  It is a cold, windy day here in the mountains of Gunnison, CO.  It will once again get into the single digits tonight.  Keep that in mind.

I was coming home today, and in my trailer park, I turned the corner to see a terrible, pitiful sight.  A horribly neglected cat was trying to walk in the bitter cold.  He/she was wet, walking with great difficulty, and every bit the definition of miserable.  I immediately stopped my truck, got out, and slowly walked up to this creature.  This cat looked me in the eyes and just said "Help Me"!  I crouched down, and talked softly to the cat, and he/she came right to my arms.  It had somehow gotten wet (I can't figure that out yet as everything is frozen solid here), and it was terribly matted and neglected.  The wet portion of this lovely cat was frozen solid--especially it's feet and tail.

When I picked Bart up--I named it tentatively--he just clung to me because I was warm, and I cradled it gently and walked to the nearest domicile.  I knocked on the door, and asked the people who answered "Whose cat is this".  The response was that the cat sort of belonged across the street, but that they don't allow it inside ever, and all they have done is put a flea collar on it, and shove a bit of food out once in awhile, and called that good.

I was told they take miserable care of this cat.  No Kidding!

Well, right or wrong, I got in my truck with Bart, and held him while I drove to my mother's home nearby in the same trailer park.  My mother is out of town for the day, but she is like me, and would take the cat in instantly to save it's life.  Wait until she gets home tonight!  Anyhow, I took Bart into the back bedroom, and examined it a bit closer.  Bart has mats the size of silver dollars on his/her backend, was wet from something, and smelled as though it has been surviving under a trailer somehow.  I immediately got a plate of cat food, and some fresh water, and I have never seen an animal actually dive into a plate of food like this.  I do mean DIVE into the food.
Bart seems to have no injuries, but is such a tangled stinky mess that I think professional care is in order starting tomorrow.  This is a long hair, all black cat, and has a wonderful temperament already.  After he/she had a chance to eat, I put down a litter box, and sat in the room with this cat.  Bart just wanted to stick close to me, and was purring like crazy.  This is probably the warmest it has been in months.  Since I have two cats already, with one that absolutely refuses to allow another cat inside, I am hoping my Mother will accept this wonderful kitty.  I am thinking she will protest, but provided that Bart gets along with Cindy (another cat that I foisted upon my Mom years ago) I have the feeling that Mom will keep Bart.  Even if she can't, since I know the two wonderful Vets in town that I have already told KittyWhiskas about, I will make sure that this wet, tangled, neglected kitty will have a warm loving home soon.

Anyhow, I guess the whole point to this long letter is that when I was finally driving home, I knew the Spirit of Christmas had finally come to me.  I feel great right now, and I think I have a wonderful new friend and perhaps family member.

Thanks for listening…I sure love belonging to the cat family!

Sincerely,
BobCat & Sam & Kimberly
