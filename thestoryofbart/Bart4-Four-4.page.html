Date:  Fri Dec 22, 2000  4:12 am
Subject:  Bart update part III

To all who have been following Bart's story,

Today was a big day for little Bart. He underwent surgery, and the vet found that part of his broken tail was dead, so Bart lost 4 inches of tail. Bart was neutered as well.  He also had the matting removed from his fur---they had to shave all of Bart except his little  face and paws! He looks like a tiny version of a male lion. Also, his teeth were cleaned and examined, and he has a fractured canine tooth that must be dealt with later. Bart was having a bit of trouble under the anesthesia, so the vet didn't want him under any longer.  I saw Bart about three minutes after surgery, and he was pretty wobbly then. I came back about hours later, and he was fine. Bart was actually rather spunky considering what he has been though. He just wants food and affection, and he is now getting plenty of both.  I can't wait until he is healthy enough to bring home.

The next step is to see what the blood workup has to say about the respiratory problem. Dr. Taylor needs to know if it is a viral infection or not, because that will impact the treatment.  Little Bart has Giardia pretty bad, but is under medication for that. Also, the blood tests will make sure that Bart is feline leukemia negative. I am praying that it is negative. Any additional prayers are most welcome.

I have posted two new pictures of little Bart...Bart the lion kitty and the other one is of Bart and me.  I am so happy to just hold him for a few seconds. He is such a loving animal---just like a tiny child. I brought him a little stuffed toy today, and he actually began to play with it a bit.

Thanks again for everyone's care and concern. You are all so special towards Bart, so I know he will be okay. What a great Christmas for both of us!

Sincerely,
BobCat & Sam & Kimberly & Bart
Date:  Sat Dec 23, 2000  10:01 pm
Subject:  Bart update part IV

My wonderful fellow club members,

I am near tears right now.  I can't tell you all how much love, concern, and caring  have been pouring out towards a wonderful little kitty. The very thing that little Bart was denied, and all he asked for, was a little love and care. Now, thanks to the members of this club, he has so much love coming in it is beyond my capacity for expression. The members of this club have surpassed words for the love they have shown towards Bart kitty, a kitty they have only seen pictures of, and kindly read my reports.

Kaye has informed me that members have expressed wishes to help with the medical bills, or send gifts, and I can assure you, they will all be welcome. The good Dr. Taylor has yet to ask for money for Bart, and I have asked for no favors. Dr. Taylor is new in this town, and he has shown me that he is more than just a worthy vet.  He is a caring, loving man who is truly interested in the health and welfare of the animals he comes in contact with. And yes, anything you can send him to help assuage this medical bill will be put to good use. I intend to contact Dr. Taylor, and we will set up a fund in Bart's name.

Bart is doing much better now--meowing at other kitties, hissing at dogs that go by, and playing with the one little toy I brought him a few days ago. He is a wonderful kitty, and he is truly the province of KittyWhiskas. He still has a long way to go, but now he has warmth, food, care, love and protection. And, Bart is very big on purring!

I guess what I am saying now is that I love you all for what you represent: True, loving human spirit as is should be. All members of this club should be very proud. We are indeed an elite group of special people.

Meowy Christmas,
BobCat & Sam & Kimberly & Bart
