Date:  Wed Dec 20, 2000  7:30 pm
Subject:  Bart Update, part II

To all,

Thanks again for all the responses----it is overwhelming, and fantastic. Thank you, thank you, thank you all.

To answer a few things:
No, my other cats haven't met Bart yet, but Yes, I am going to try to introduce him into my home. I have a girl friend that lives in the Philippines, and she has requested that I keep little Bart so she could have a cat of her own when she moves here. I am hoping it works out with Bart here, but if not, then I think my mother will have another wonderful kitty.
Okay, now onto important news. First, the good news: Bart is eating like a little pig and scarfing down solid food now. His eyes are much brighter, and he is apparently loosening up as far as his terrible cold goes. He is now under medication to get rid of the Giardia, and that should bring his diarrhea under control. He is still being hydrated via IV because of the severe diarrhea. Now, the not so good news: Upon closer examination, and some preliminary clean up, the vet discovered that Bart's tail is broken close to the middle. I am suspecting not only gross neglect, but also regular abuse. I  am not sure what I can do about this, but we know who had him before, and all his ills and problems are being well documented. I will keep everyone up to date about this facet of Bart's story as well.

Anyhow, tomorrow, the vet is going to cautiously put Bart under anesthesia, fix the tail break, and do some clean up of Bart's horribly matted fur. The vet says he is going to try to clean Bart's teeth, but that Bart doesn't have many teeth left. Much of this depends upon how Bart is doing under the anesthesia, and the vet assured me that he will be very closely monitored.
I did mention to the vet, Dr. Taylor, that Bart's progress is being closely monitored by KittyWhiskas—the very club that thought enough of him to send him a free tee shirt. He is a very good man, and I believe that he is taking a professional approach along with a personal interest. Bart will be fine, I am just praying that there is no problems with the anesthesia.

I have posted two new pictures of Bart--one in his hospital bed (be sure to look at those intense eyes that are so much brighter after only three days) and the other one a picture of Bart thinking of jumping down from his hospital bed, but not really wanting to very much. He leaned on me today, and purred like crazy. I am already in love with him.

All of you can expect daily updates about Bart. After all, since I am the custodian of the special KittyWhiskas Christmas Cat, and that KW members have adopted Bart as a club kitty, I must do my duty and report.

You know, all of you are very special people. Thank you all for your support and interest.

Sincerely,
BobCat & Sam & Kimberly & Bart
