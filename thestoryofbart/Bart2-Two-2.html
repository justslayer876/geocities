Subject:  Bart's first night & First bath

To all,

First, I would like to thank all who wrote for the very heartfelt messages that have come in response to my story of Bart, the horribly neglected cat.  I can feel the love from everyone towards this little waif. At least one little kitty is warm and fed tonight that was miserable and starving today.

I posted a picture of Bart right after his first bath tonight. I was able to clip a few of the mats off of his legs, but the rest will have to come off at the vet's tomorrow. Also, Bart had some very sticky, oily substance on him/her and I think that will have to be professionally removed as well. I found bits of insulation on him/her which tells me that this cat was living under a trailer and sleeping in the insulation. However, I have to say, that for a kitty that has only been with us only a few hours, to give it a bath, this cat was very good about it. I am not saying he liked it , but I have seen much, much worse attitudes when it comes to cats and water. Mom and I got some of the crud off of him/her, but by no means all. This is going to be one great kitty.  I  will keep you all posted as to Bart's progress. Thank you all once again. Bart is now a province of all of us at KittyWhiskas.

BobCat & Sam & Kimberly
Date:  Tue Dec 19, 2000  7:48 pm
Subject:  Bart's Update

To all,

I can feel all the love going out to Bart, and all the good wishes towards me.  This experience just keeps getting better and better, and now I have to share the good news about Bart, and rest assured, I will tell him all about your responses.

Okay, I took Bart, which is now officially his name, into the vet on Monday morning only about 18 hours after his rescue from the cold and neglect. The vet estimates that Bart is over 4 years old. I don't know how he has survived that long. The vet says he was on his last days if I hadn't brought him in. He had all kinds of worms, some upper respiratory problems, and a very bad cold. Also, due to his extreme neglect and poor diet, he has lost some teeth. As the vet put it, he has some "issues" to be resolved. He was able to eat only some canned food, and he was terribly dehydrated. Also, he has diarrhea, probably from the worms and possibly giardia.

Mom and I visited him (her new grandchild) today, and he has taken some solid food, not much, but some. That is a good sign. Also, while I was there, he started to clean himself, which pleased the vet very much because this is also a good sign. He hasn't been cleaned up yet, or had the mats in his fur taken out yet because the vet says that he is already under enough stress from the medication, the complete changes around him, and also having to be re-hydrated via IV. In a few days, after his system and his terrible cold is better, then some cosmetic things can happen-like a bath again, and I am afraid that he will have to be shaved in order to get the matting out of his fur.  Last  night, only one night after his rescue, the temperature here dropped to -15 degrees F. Bitter, bitter cold. Killing cold. At 10:30am this morning, it was still only -4 degrees F.

I am going to keep a photo log of his progress, and I promise to continue to keep everyone here at KW posted. As I said before, Bart is now the province of all of us at KW. I just happen to be the lucky custodian. Merry Christmas to All out there, and to you humans too.

BobCat & Sam & Kimberly & (now) Bart
