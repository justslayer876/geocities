Date:  Mon Dec 25, 2000  12:45 pm
Subject:  Christmas Eve update on Bart KW mascot

Dear everyone,

I have just posted two new pictures of little Bart.  I titled one "Bart the proud kitty" because he had an air of the wonderful self assured "cat-ness" that we all love about our kitties. Please do not be alarmed that his hospital bed looks messed in—I can assure you that his bed gets changed and cleaned several times a day. He still is having problems with the Giardia thing, so he can "mess" his cage in an instant.  The next picture is wiith his Christmas toy, and a clean bed.

Bart is doing much better, but there are still a few issues to be resolved before he can come home. His URI needs to be settled, and the bowel problem definitely needs to settle down. It has been eight days in the hospital so far, but the change is wondrous, thanks to the care and professional attention of Dr. Taylor and staff.

I will visit Bart again today, and play with him for a bit. He is just such a loving kitty.  All he ever asked for was to be loved and to give love and affection back, but was denied that until a fateful Sunday afternoon around 4pm, December 17th, 2000.  From that point on, Bart became the province of KW, and now a club mascot. I love you all for this.

Sincerely,
BobCat & Sam & Kimberly & (especially) Bart
